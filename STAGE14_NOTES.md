# Stage 14 — Advanced Tactical Intelligence (revision 2)

## Integrated changes
- Corrected slider geometry validation for absolute-pin, skewer and discovered-attack patterns. A rook can no longer be reported as pinning along a diagonal (and bishops/rooks are constrained to their real movement rays).
- Added verified **mate-in-one** detection. Candidate moves are generated by `ChessEngine.legalMoves`, applied through `ChessEngine.apply`, and accepted only when the resulting position is checkmate according to `ChessEngine.result`.
- Added a conservative **possible tactical sacrifice** signal. It requires a legal move, a legal opponent capture of the moved piece, and a forcing legal continuation (check or capture of a rook/queen). It is explicitly a calculation prompt, not a soundness verdict.
- Tactical squares are now highlighted directly on the game board as well as listed in the Tactical Intelligence panel.
- Added regression tests for invalid rook-pin geometry and a verified mate-in-one position.

## Existing tactical coverage retained
Fork patterns, absolute pins, skewers, discovered-attack setups, hanging/undefended pieces, under-attack warnings, legal one-ply checking/capture candidates, and near-promotion signals.

## Important limits
- Fork, skewer, discovered-attack, under-attack and promotion findings are positional heuristics; they do not prove a winning combination.
- The sacrifice signal is deliberately narrow and can miss sound sacrifices; it does not calculate a full exchange tree or prove compensation.
- This revision detects an available mate in one, not multi-move forced mates, strategic mating threats, or general tactical traps. Those require the multi-ply search planned for Stage 15 / Stockfish.
- The tactical analyzer runs from the current local position and does not use global game statistics.
- The Stockfish Android executable is still absent from this source ZIP. The existing UCI adapter reports this honestly; no engine evaluation is fabricated.
